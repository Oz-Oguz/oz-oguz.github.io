I am an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Computer Engineering](https://w3.cs.bilkent.edu.tr/) at [Bilkent University](https://w3.bilkent.edu.tr/bilkent/), where I lead the **[Learning for Intelligent Robotic Agents (LiRA) Lab](https://lira-lab.github.io/)**.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems and the Uni. of Stuttgart (Learning and Intelligent Systems / Machine Learning & Robotics Lab) working with [Marc Toussaint](https://www.user.tu-berlin.de//mtoussai//), affiliated with the [Cluster of Excellence: IntCDC](https://www.intcdc.uni-stuttgart.de/). I received my Ph.D. (*summa cum laude*) from the [Technical University of Munich (TUM)](https://www.ce.cit.tum.de/en/lsr/home/). I studied Computer Science at the [Uni. of British Columbia (UBC)](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/) and [Koç University](https://rml.ku.edu.tr/).

### Research Interests & LiRA Lab
My research sits at the intersection of **Robotics, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence**. At the **[LiRA Lab](https://lira-lab.github.io/)**, we specialize in building active, embodied AI agents capable of perceiving, reasoning about, and interacting seamlessly with the physical world:
- **Physical Intelligence & Embodied AI:** Grounding vision-language models (VLMs) for dexterous manipulation.
- **Multi-Arm, Dexterous & Contact-Rich Control:** Optimization-augmented reactive control and dynamic tool manipulation.
- **Task and Motion Planning (TAMP):** Logic-Geometric Programming (LGP), sequential manipulation, and hybrid planners.
- **Robot Learning:** Deep reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and generative policy synthesis.

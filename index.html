I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Engineering at Bilkent University, where I lead the Learning for Intelligent Robotic Agents (LiRA) Lab.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems and the Uni. of Stuttgart (Learning and Intelligent Systems / Machine Learning & Robotics Lab) working with Marc Toussaint, affiliated with the Cluster of Excellence: IntCDC. I received my Ph.D. (summa cum laude) from the Technical University of Munich (TUM). I studied Computer Science at the Uni. of British Columbia (UBC) and Koç University.

Research Interests & LiRA Lab

My research sits at the intersection of Robotics, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence. At the LiRA Lab, we specialize in building active, embodied AI agents capable of perceiving, reasoning about, and interacting seamlessly with the physical world:

  • Physical Intelligence & Embodied AI: Grounding vision-language models (VLMs) for dexterous manipulation.
  • Multi-Arm, Dexterous & Contact-Rich Control: Optimization-augmented reactive control and dynamic tool manipulation.
  • Task and Motion Planning (TAMP): Logic-Geometric Programming (LGP), sequential manipulation, and hybrid planners.
  • Robot Learning: Deep reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and generative policy synthesis.

Prospective Students & Researchers

  • Post-Doc, Ph.D., and M.Sc. positions are available in the LiRA Lab.
  • Highly motivated undergraduate and graduate students interested in embodied AI, robot learning, deep perception, and physical intelligence are encouraged to reach out via email with their CV and transcript.
